# opencineui (candidate)

Proposed shared design system: Swift `MonitorPresentation` and `MonitorUI`, Compose
`monitor-ui`. Status `candidate`: a local repository being created by UI-01/UI-02 from
the frozen Pocket donor. No app consumes it on its baseline yet; its edges are not live
dependencies. Update `refs`, products and edge confidence once it has a reviewed commit.

Route here: shared tokens, controls, panels and layouts, after adoption. Until then the
owning source is `openpocketcine`.
