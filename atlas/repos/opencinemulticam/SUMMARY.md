# opencinemulticam (candidate)

Neutral multicam engine from MULTI-01: entry membership and stable slots, group start/stop plans
(strict or eligible-subset, deduplicated by control session, per-target outcomes, no
compensation), editor leases, resource admission with an injected qualification limit,
per-entry recovery budgets and a saveable layout, in Swift and as a Kotlin/JVM mirror (Gradle
module `:multicam`, `com.opencapture.opencine:multicam`). Depends only on OpenCineCore. Status
`candidate`: a local repository; no app consumes it yet (MULTI-02 adapts Pocket and Nikon).
Build and test instructions: its own `AGENTS.md`.
